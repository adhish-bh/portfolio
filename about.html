About

I build to understand how ideas become working software.

How I work

I prefer projects that are clear, useful, and thoughtfully structured. Rather than rushing to add features, I focus on understanding the logic, breaking the problem into smaller parts, and improving the result through testing and iteration.

What I study

Alongside programming, I have studied Agile, SDLC, SRS documentation, object-oriented programming, UML diagrams, WBS, and Gantt charts. These concepts help me think about requirements, planning, implementation, testing, and maintenance as connected parts of the same system.

What I build

My projects include frontend websites, Angular applications, Java desktop systems, Python programs, database-connected management systems, IoT prototypes, and introductory data analysis work.

Where I am heading

I’m expanding from foundational web and software projects into Angular, automation, APIs, machine learning, and full-stack development. My goal is simple: keep building stronger projects and become a more capable and dependable developer.
